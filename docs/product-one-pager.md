# Product One Pager

## Product Vision

An AI-powered website that generates personalized mock tests for Grade 9 final examinations. The platform helps students practise exam-style questions, identify weak topics, and prepare from anywhere.

## Problem Statement

Grade 9 students often struggle to find enough relevant practice questions that match their school syllabus and examination format. Existing worksheets may be repetitive, outdated, or unable to provide immediate feedback.

This product makes exam preparation easier by allowing students to generate customised mock tests online without needing to attend additional classes or travel to a learning centre. It also helps students understand mistakes and focus on topics that require improvement.

## Target Users

The primary users are Grade 9 students preparing for their final examinations.

Students will use the website to:

- Practise exam-style questions
- Review different subjects and topics
- Identify strengths and weaknesses
- Receive immediate results and explanations
- Prepare independently from home or school

## Key User Needs

- Relevant practice questions that match Grade 9 subjects, syllabus, difficulty, and exam format
- Immediate feedback and explanations after answering
- Personalized preparation focused on weaker subjects and topics

## MVP Features

- Registration and login
- Subject selection
- Topic selection
- AI mock test generation
- Difficulty selection
- Question type selection
- Test settings
- Interactive test interface
- Automatic marking
- Results summary
- Answer explanations
- Performance by topic
- Test history
- Progress dashboard
